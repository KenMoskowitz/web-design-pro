About

A boutique practice, with the depth of a much larger firm.

Cornerstone was founded on a quiet observation: most estate plans are sold, not designed. Families end up with a binder full of boilerplate that nobody — including them — fully understands.

We do the opposite. Every plan starts with a real conversation, gets drafted by an attorney (never a paralegal in the background), and is reviewed annually so it actually keeps up with your life. Our founding partner is an ACTEC Fellow with 18 years of estate practice across California and federal jurisdictions.
