About

Three generations. One promise: do the job right.

D&H started in 1972 when Don and Harold opened a one-truck shop out of a garage on the north side. The trucks are nicer now and there are more of them — but the rule hasn't changed: do the job the way you'd want it done at your own mother's house.

Today the third generation runs the company. Every technician is NATE-certified, background-checked, drug-tested, and put through a 90-day in-house apprenticeship before they're trusted in a customer's home alone. We pay our techs well so they stay — the average tenure on our crew is 11 years.
