About

Sixty-five Tucson summers and counting.

D&H opened in Tucson in 1959 and has run on the same promise ever since: do the job the way you'd want it done at your own mother's house. We've outlasted three generations of AC technology and one whole century turning over — but the rule hasn't changed.

Today the company runs from two Tucson locations on Evans Boulevard and Oracle Road, with technicians who are NATE-certified, Pearl-Contractor-trained, and on call 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. We service Tucson, Marana, Oro Valley, Vail, Green Valley, Sahuarita, and the surrounding communities of Southern Arizona.
